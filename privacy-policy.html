Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

1. About This Application

This privacy policy applies to the Instagram messaging bot ("Aria") operated by Awez Waghu, Strategic Growth Head, on behalf of professional structural consultancy communications. Aria is an AI-powered assistant that responds to direct messages received on a professional Instagram account.

2. Information We Collect

When you send a direct message to this Instagram account, we may process the following information:

• Your Instagram username and user ID

• The content of messages you send to this account

• The timestamp of your messages

We do not collect, store, or share any personal data beyond what is necessary to respond to your message.

3. How We Use Your Information

The information collected is used solely to:

• Generate and send automated replies to your direct messages

• Provide relevant information about structural consultancy services

• Improve the quality of responses over time

We do not sell, trade, or transfer your personal information to any third parties.

4. Data Storage

Messages processed by Aria are not permanently stored. Each message is processed in real-time to generate a response and is not retained in any database after the response is sent.

5. Third-Party Services

This application uses the following third-party services:

• Instagram Graph API (Meta Platforms Inc.) — to receive and send messages

• Anthropic Claude API — to generate intelligent responses to messages

Both services have their own privacy policies which govern their use of data.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

• Stop receiving automated responses by ceasing to message this account

• Request information about what data we hold about you

• Request deletion of any data associated with your interactions
